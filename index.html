THE MECHANICS REVEALED

What did the dashboard actually prove?

This demonstration creates the appearance of progress using fictional balances, activity and a growing network. None of those displays represents profit, investment performance or money held for members.

In a pyramid scheme, money paid by newer participants is used to pay earlier participants. The structure needs a continuous supply of new entrants. Recruitment eventually becomes mathematically impossible: the number of people needed grows exponentially, while the pool of potential participants is finite. Most participants therefore cannot recover what they put in.

Warning signs: returns depend on recruitment; earnings are presented as guaranteed; the product has little independent value; members are pressured to recruit; and withdrawals depend on fresh contributions.

Key lesson: a polished website, impressive dashboard, testimonials and visible payouts are not proof of a legitimate business. Ask where the revenue comes from and whether the model works without recruiting new members.
